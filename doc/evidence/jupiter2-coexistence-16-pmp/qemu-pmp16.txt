  Early completed the IMA assertion and powered down.
  Late timed out at 180s. UART contains root-config PASS and configure-return
  rc=0 on hart 4, then initialization of the other 14 secondary harts.
  Counting from "Primary CPU initialized", all 15 secondary harts logged
  configure-return rc=0. Both the opening and closing text of the SM
  initialization message occurred 14 times. The OP-TEE initialization
  completion list contained harts 1, 2, 3 and 5–15.
  Hart 4 was past HSM request delivery and PMP configuration. The missing
  SM completion precedes initial HSM finish and the entry to OP-TEE.
  Five exact-input late reruns passed the full IMA assertion.
  Boot-only checks reached 16 CPUs in 24/24 runs, and in 10/10 additional
  runs bound to one host CPU. No QMP snapshot was taken during the first
  failing run.

Follow-up using the original trace-enabled firmware
  The original QEMU, firmware, Core, late DTB, Linux and initramfs
  matched the SHA-256 values listed above. The argument order and UART
  progress reader matched scripts/tests/jupiter2-ftpm-ima-qemu.sh.
  QMP was connected for register capture if a boot remained pending.
  All 48 normal-affinity attempts reached the Linux 16-CPU marker.
  The timing stayed below the first scheduled register capture.
  Each boot logged configure-return rc=0 and the complete SM marker
  for all 15 secondary harts. OP-TEE's UART occasionally interleaved
  messages, so its completion line count alone can undercount.
  The original failure had all 15 configure-return markers and 14 SM
  markers. This places it in the interval matched by KSB-096.
  With affinity restricted to two host CPUs, two boots reached Linux
  and the third timed out at 180s. UART reported "Error starting secondary
  hart 7". At 25, 55, 120 and 165 seconds, hart 7 was in
  sbi_hsm_hart_wait (PC 0x10201759c) and hart 0 in wait_secondary
  (PC 0x1710001f8). The UART byte count stayed at 63197.
  The affected hart had no PMP configure-return marker in that run.
  This matches the failed-start stage observed for KSB-097.
